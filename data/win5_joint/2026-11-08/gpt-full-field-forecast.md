## Method limitations
The full-field distribution is a transparent qualitative scoring distribution based on the JRA racecard's recent form, class evidence, distance/course suitability, weight and jockey context where available. Scores are normalized within each race. It is not a fitted or backtested statistical model and should be treated as low-to-medium reliability. The current racecard odds are reported separately and were not used to calculate the GPT estimates.
